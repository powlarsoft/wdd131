1. Site Name

3Core Tech

The name 3Core Tech was selected to represent a modern technology company built around several major technology areas. “3Core” suggests a strong foundational core of expertise, while “Tech” clearly signals the company’s focus on technology solutions.

The name reflects the company’s umbrella approach to Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, Robotics, Solar Technology, and Smart Technology. It is short, memorable, and suitable for a professional technology business based in Nigeria.

Optional domain concept: 3coretech.ng or similar (availability not verified for this student project; no domain has been purchased).

2. Site Purpose

The 3Core Tech website introduces a Nigerian technology company that develops and provides practical technology solutions for individuals and businesses. Its focus areas include Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, Robotics, Solar Technology, Smart Technology, Business Automation, and GPS & Fleet Technology.

The site serves potential customers such as small business owners, entrepreneurs, organizations, and technology partners. Visitors can learn what 3Core Tech does, explore its major technology areas and planned services, view selected project concepts, understand the company’s vision, and contact the team.

The website is primarily an informational business presence. It is not a full e-commerce platform. Contact is available via email (3core.ng@gmail.com) and a simple contact form.

In addition to serving real visitors, the site demonstrates skills learned in WDD 131: valid HTML, CSS, responsive design, JavaScript, accessibility, SEO basics, and professional development practices.

3. Scenarios

The following scenarios represent common questions visitors may have when arriving at the site:

Scenario 1

What AI and software solutions does 3Core Tech provide for businesses?

A small business owner or entrepreneur visits the site looking for AI assistants, chatbots, business process automation, custom applications, or related software services. They want a clear overview of the technology areas and service categories offered.

Scenario 2

How can I contact 3Core Tech about a technology project?

A visitor has reviewed the services and project concepts and wants a simple, reliable way to reach the company. They expect to find an email address and a contact form so they can discuss a potential project.

4. Color Schema

The color palette communicates technology, innovation, and professionalism. Deep navy conveys reliability and engineering strength; electric cyan provides modern energy and highlights interactive elements; soft off-white keeps content areas clean and readable.

Usage

  • Primary (#0A1F44) — Main headings, navigation, hero sections, footer, important text
  • Accent (#00D4FF) — Buttons, links, icons, highlights, interactive elements, borders
  • Background (#F8FAFC) — Page backgrounds and section areas
  • Text (#1E293B) — Body copy and primary content
  • Muted (#64748B) — Secondary text and labels
  • Cards (#FFFFFF) — Content cards and panels

All combinations were selected for sufficient contrast to support readability and basic accessibility requirements.

5. Typography

Two modern sans-serif fonts are used to keep the design clean, professional, and highly readable. No decorative fonts are included.

Heading Sample — Poppins
Body text sample — Inter. This paragraph demonstrates the body font used for descriptions, paragraphs, forms, and general content throughout the site.

Headings: Poppins (600 / 500)  |  Body: Inter (400 / 500)  |  Fallback: system-ui, sans-serif

Heading Font — Poppins

  • Used for main headings, section headings, navigation, and important labels
  • Weights: 600 (semi-bold) for primary headings, 500 for secondary headings and nav

Body Font — Inter

  • Used for paragraphs, descriptions, form labels, and general content
  • Weights: 400 (regular) and 500 (medium) for emphasis

Both fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. A system font stack is provided as fallback to ensure readability if the web fonts fail to load.
